International Programs Gallery

Marketing Poster Series

The task was to create a series of posters to advertise Pepperdine's highly acclaimed International Programs, in an exhibition that students pass everyday. The main programs Pepperdine hosts over the school year feature black and white photos of famous landmarks in each location with marble patterns in colors from the country's flag. After finishing the first seven, the goal was to create something that complemented the images without being too busy—the solution was to highlight the shape of each country in the same marble pattern.
